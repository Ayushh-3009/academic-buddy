Take a breath, Alex! You've got this. Let's imagine an arcade claw machine: 🕹️

The Key Difference

1. Competitive: A fake coin jams the slot where real coins go (competes for the active site). If you dump 100 real coins in, you can out-compete it!

2. Non-competitive: Someone kicks the plug out of the wall (binds to an allosteric site). Doesn't matter how many coins you have—the machine changes shape and stops working.
